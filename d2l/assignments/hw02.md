## Part A — Find the equilibrium saturated thickness (40 points)

### Physical model

Consider an unconfined aquifer extending from a groundwater divide to a stream.
At equilibrium, recharge collected across the aquifer balances discharge to the
stream plus pumping. Expressed per unit aquifer width, define the residual:

$$
f(h) = \frac{K(h^2-h_s^2)}{2L} + q_p - RL
$$

where:

- $h$ is saturated thickness at the divide (m),
- $h_s$ is saturated thickness at the stream (m),
- $K$ is hydraulic conductivity (m/d),
- $L$ is distance from the divide to the stream (m),
- $R$ is recharge (m/d), and
- $q_p$ is pumping per unit width (m²/d).

The equilibrium value of $h$ makes $f(h)=0$.

Use:

```python
K = 11.0             # m/d
L = 1100.0           # m
H_STREAM = 7.5       # m
RECHARGE = 4.5e-4    # m/d
PUMPING = 0.060      # m2/d per unit aquifer width
```
