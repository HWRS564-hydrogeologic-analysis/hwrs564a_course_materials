## Part 2 — Estimate pore-water volume (10 points)

Calculate the bulk aquifer volume:

$$
V_{bulk} = L W b
$$

and the estimated pore-water volume:

$$
V_{water} = n V_{bulk}
$$

where $L$ is length, $W$ is width, $b$ is saturated thickness, and $n$ is
porosity.

with descriptive labels and units. Use scientific notation or digit grouping
so the magnitudes are easy to compare.

## Part 3 — Calculate groundwater flow (15 points)

First convert hydraulic conductivity to metres per day:

$$
K_{m/d} = K_{m/s}(86{,}400\;s/d)
$$

Then calculate the magnitude of the hydraulic gradient:

$$
i = \frac{h_{up}-h_{down}}{\Delta l}
$$

and Darcy flow through the vertical cross-section:

$$
Q = K i A, \qquad A = bW
$$

Store the intermediate and final results using these names:

- `hydraulic_conductivity_m_d`
- `hydraulic_gradient`
- `flow_area_m2`
- `specific_discharge_m_d`
- `volumetric_flow_m3_d`

flow as a positive magnitude; describe its direction in words.
